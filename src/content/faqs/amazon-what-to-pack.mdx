---
question: "What should I pack for the Amazon?"
category: "amazon"
order: 3
---

Light, quick-drying **long-sleeved shirts and trousers**, a rain jacket, insect repellent, sun protection, a hat, a torch (flashlight) and binoculars if you have them. It's a rainforest, so expect some rain whatever the month. Most lodges lend rubber boots — we'll confirm for yours.
