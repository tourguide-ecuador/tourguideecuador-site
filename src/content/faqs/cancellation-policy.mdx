question: "What is your cancellation policy?"
category: "booking"
order: 3
---

**Confirming your booking.** A day tour is confirmed once payment is received — by credit card, PayPal or bank transfer. We always suggest a quick call, email or WhatsApp the day before your tour to make sure every detail is right. It isn't obligatory, but it helps.

**Day tours — if you need to cancel a confirmed booking:**

- More than 7 days before the tour: 10% of the tour price.
- 3–7 days before: 50% of the tour price.
- Within 72 hours of the start: no refund.

**Changing your date.** Changes made more than 6 days before the original date are free of charge, as long as the new date is within 2 months.

**Galápagos cruises and multi-day programmes** follow the boat or lodge operator's own policy, which is usually stricter the closer you get to departure. We send you the exact terms with your quote, before you pay anything.

Whatever you book, we strongly recommend travel insurance — it's well worth the peace of mind.
