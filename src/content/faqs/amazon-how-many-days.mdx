question: "How many days do I need in the Amazon?"
category: "amazon"

We'd suggest **at least 3 nights (4 days)**. Most Amazon lodges are reached by a short flight from Quito to Coca followed by a motorised canoe ride, so the first and last days are mostly travel. With 4 nights you get more time on the trails, lagoons and canopy towers — and a better chance of spotting wildlife. Tell us your dates and we'll suggest the right lodge and length.
